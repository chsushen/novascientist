# NovaScientist Product Vision & Positioning

## 1. Core Positioning
**NovaScientist is Evidence-First Autonomous Research Infrastructure.**

It is designed to structure, accelerate, and automate scientific investigation pipelines while retaining human scientific review and oversight.

### What NovaScientist Is:
- A systematic research execution platform that decomposes questions into formal scientific contracts.
- An evidence-grounded framework linking empirical results directly to published DOI citations.
- A deterministic multi-seed benchmarking suite enforcing pre-split AST isolation.
- An end-to-end publisher compiling publication-ready IEEE Transactions PDFs with verified provenance.

### What NovaScientist Is NOT:
- NovaScientist is NOT an "AI that writes papers" or a tool for fabricating ungrounded synthetic research.
- NovaScientist does NOT replace human scientists; it provides the infrastructure to execute, validate, and document hypothesis-driven empirical workflows.

---

## 2. Target Audience
- **AI & Machine Learning Research Labs**: Conducting multi-seed reproducible empirical evaluations.
- **University Academic Groups**: Establishing baseline comparisons with verified literature citations.
- **Enterprise R&D Teams**: Automating benchmarking and technical report synthesis across models and datasets.
